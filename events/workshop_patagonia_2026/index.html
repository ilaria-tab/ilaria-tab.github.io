Advances in Patagonian glaciers research

Two-day workshop on connecting Field Observations, Modelling and Global Perspectives.

Register interest

About the workshop

Patagonia hosts one of the largest glacierized regions outside the polar areas and is undergoing some of the most rapid glacier changes on Earth. This two-day workshop brings together Chilean and international researchers to present recent advances in Patagonian glacier observations, field measurements, numerical modelling, and future projections, while placing Patagonian glaciers in a broader international context. Organized around the visit of researchers from Friedrich-Alexander-Universität Erlangen-Nürnberg (FAU, Germany), the workshop aims to foster scientific collaboration, exchange ideas across disciplines, and engage undergraduate and graduate students with current developments in glacier science.

Program

Scientific sessions

19 October 2026

📍 Auditorium Alamiro Robledo, Facultad de Ciencias Físicas y Matemáticas, Universidad de Concepción

Session 1

Current state of Patagonian glaciers

An overview of the current status of Patagonian glaciers, including their recent evolution, the atmospheric processes controlling surface mass balance, and the role of proglacial lakes in glacier change.

Session 2

Ice thickness observations in Patagonia

Recent advances in mapping glacier thickness and subglacial topography using airborne geophysical surveys, together with first results from the 2026 Patagonia field campaign.

Session 3

Emerging observations in Patagonia

New observational techniques and instrumentation for glaciological research, complemented by student presentations showcasing recent field measurements and field campaigns in Patagonia.

20 October 2026

📍 Auditorium Alamiro Robledo, Facultad de Ciencias Físicas y Matemáticas, Universidad de Concepción

Session 4

Advances in glacier modelling and future hazards

How numerical models are advancing our understanding of glacier evolution from regional to global scales, with applications to future water resources, glacier hazards, and climate change.

Session 5

Modelling and future perspectives in Patagonia

Current developments in modelling Patagonian glaciers, their representation in international climate assessments, and an open discussion on future scientific challenges and collaborative opportunities.
