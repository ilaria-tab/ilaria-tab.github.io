Advances in Patagonian glaciers research

Connecting Field Observations, Modelling and Global Perspectives.

A two-day workshop bringing together observations, modelling, and global perspectives to advance Patagonian glacier research.

Register interest

About the workshop

Patagonia hosts one of the largest glacierized regions outside the polar areas and is experiencing some of the most rapid glacier changes on Earth. Understanding these changes requires integrating field observations and numerical modelling across a wide range of spatial and temporal scales.

This workshop aims to bring together researchers working on different aspects of Patagonian glacier research to share recent advances, discuss emerging challenges, and strengthen collaborations within the glaciological community. The scientific programme spans from observations and field instrumentation to glacier modelling and future projections, while placing Patagonian glaciers within a broader international context.

The workshop is organized around the visit of researchers from Friedrich-Alexander-Universität Erlangen-Nürnberg (FAU, Germany), who will be carrying out fieldwork in Patagonia immediately beforehand. Their participation provides a unique opportunity to exchange ideas between international and Chilean researchers, foster new collaborations, and engage undergraduate and graduate students with current developments in glacier science.
