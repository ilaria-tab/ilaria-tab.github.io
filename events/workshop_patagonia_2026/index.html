Advances in Patagonian glaciers research

Two-day workshop on connecting Field Observations, Modelling and Global Perspectives.

Registration closed View program

About the workshop

Patagonia hosts one of the largest glacierized regions outside the polar areas and is undergoing some of the most rapid glacier changes on Earth. This two-day workshop brings together Chilean and international researchers to present recent advances in Patagonian glacier observations, field measurements, numerical modelling, and future projections, while placing Patagonian glaciers in a broader international context. Organized around the visit of researchers from Friedrich-Alexander-Universität Erlangen-Nürnberg (FAU, Germany), the workshop aims to foster scientific collaboration, exchange ideas across disciplines, and engage undergraduate and graduate students with current developments in glacier science.

Mobility grants

A limited number of mobility grants will be available for outstanding undergraduate and graduate students, as well as ECS participants from other regions of Chile, who are selected to attend the workshop.

The grants consist of reimbursement for travel and accommodation expenses, with a maximum total of (per person):

  • CLP $100,000 for participants from regions other than Aysén and Magallanes.
  • CLP $250,000 for participants from Aysén or Magallanes.

Applicants must request the grant when applying to the workshop. After acceptance, selected applicants will be contacted with details on the reimbursement process and required documentation.

Grants are limited and not guaranteed, and reimbursement is subject to the submission of the corresponding receipts and the applicable administrative requirements.
