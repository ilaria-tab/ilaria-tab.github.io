About

I am a glaciologist and physicist specialized in numerical modelling of glaciers and ice sheets, ice-ocean interactions, and climate-driven glacier change. My work combines field observations, remote sensing, and three-dimensional thermo-mechanical ice-flow modelling, with applications in Greenland, Antarctica, and Patagonia.

Current position

Assistant Professor, Department of Geophysics, Universidad de Concepción, Chile.

Teaching

Numerical Methods in Geophysics, Global Physical Climatology, Advanced Numerical Methods in Geophysics, and Foundations of Research.

Research interests

Ice-sheet and glacier modelling

Numerical modelling of the dynamics and evolution of terrestrial ice bodies across temporal and spatial scales.

Ice-climate interactions

Ice-ocean, ice-atmosphere, ice-solid Earth interactions, paleoclimatology, and climate change impacts.

Patagonia, Greenland & Antarctica

Field observations, remote sensing, glacier hydrology, GLOFs, and tipping points in the Earth system.
