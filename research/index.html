Research

Glacier and ice-sheet dynamics in a changing climate

Numerical modelling, field observations, and remote sensing across Patagonia, Greenland, and Antarctica.

Research lines

Ice-flow modelling

Three-dimensional thermo-mechanical modelling of glaciers and ice sheets across different temporal and spatial scales.

Ice-climate interactions

Ice-ocean, ice-atmosphere, ice-solid Earth interactions, paleoclimatology, and climate-change impacts.

Glacier hydrology and hazards

Glacier hydrology, supraglacial lakes, Glacial Lake Outburst Floods (GLOFs), and tipping points in the Earth system.
