Research interests

Ice-flow modelling

Numerical modelling of glacier and ice-sheet dynamics over different temporal and spatial scales.

Ice-climate interactions

Ice-ocean, ice-atmosphere, and ice-solid Earth interactions, paleoclimatology, and climate change.

Glacier hydrology and hazards

Glacier hydrology, Patagonian ice fields, Greenland and Antarctic ice sheets, GLOFs, and glacier-related hazards.
